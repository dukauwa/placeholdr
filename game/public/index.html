STICKMOUFLAGE

Paint your white body to blend into the stage. Just like a chameleon — but stickier.

How to play
  • Hiders are white stick figures in a 3D world. During the prep phase, sample surface colors with the eyedropper and paint your body to blend in. Pick a clever spot and a pose that breaks your silhouette.
  • Seekers wait blindfolded, then hunt in first person. Click to shoot paintballs — hit a hider to tag them. Ammo is limited and regenerates slowly, so don't spray.
  • Move: WASD, mouse to look (click to capture the mouse), Shift run, Space jump (twice = double jump). Push into a climbable wall to cling, then W/S to climb, Space to leap off.
  • Paint: F opens the palette and frees the mouse. Left-click your body to paint, Alt/right-click any surface to sample its color, wheel = brush size, G fill, Z undo, X clear.
  • Pose: R cycles poses, 1–7 picks one directly (stand, crouch, ball, T-pose, lie, sit, star).
